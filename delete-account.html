Delete Your ExpensePilot Account

This page explains how to delete your ExpensePilot account (Android app by Nilesh Yadav) and the data stored on our servers. You don't need the app installed to make a request.

Option 1: In the App

  1. If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first in the Google Play Store (Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ExpensePilot → Cancel).
  2. Open ExpensePilot and go to More → Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm and choose your Google account. Your account and synced data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: Request by Email

Send an email to support@expensepilot.in with the subject "Delete my ExpensePilot account", from the Google account (email address) you use in ExpensePilot. We will delete your account and data and confirm by email.

What Is Deleted

  • Your ExpensePilot sign-in account (linked to your Google account)
  • Your profile (name, email address, profile photo link and subscription status)
  • All synced data: expenses and income, categories, budgets, category limits, bills and recurring entries, savings goals and their contributions

What Is Kept

  • Nothing on our servers once deletion is complete.
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically under Firebase's retention settings.
  • Data stored only on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Your Google Play purchase history is kept by Google, as for every app.

How Long It Takes

Deletion in the app is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days, usually much sooner, and we confirm by email when it's done.
