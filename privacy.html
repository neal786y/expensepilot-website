Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 7 November 2026

ExpensePilot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. We understand that your financial information is sensitive, and we treat it with the utmost care and respect. This policy explains what the ExpensePilot Android app collects, why, and how you stay in control.

How We Handle Your Data

Offline-First Architecture: ExpensePilot is designed with privacy at its core. Your expenses, income, budgets, bills, goals and categories are stored locally on your device by default. You can use the app without an account, and we don't receive your financial data unless you sign in and use cloud sync.

Cloud Sync (Optional, Pro): If you sign in with Google and subscribe to Pro, your financial data is stored in Google Firebase so it can sync across your devices. This is entirely optional and at your discretion.

What Data We Collect

  • Locally Stored Data: Expenses, income, budgets, category limits, bills, savings goals, categories and preferences remain on your device
  • Google Account Information (if you sign in): Your name, email address and profile photo from Google, and an account ID, used to show your account and link your synced data to it
  • Cloud Data (if you use Pro sync): Your entries, categories, budgets, category limits, bills and goals, so they sync across your devices
  • Purchase Information: Your Pro subscription status from Google Play
  • Usage Analytics: Which screens and features are used (for example "an expense was added" or "a bill was marked paid"), collected with Google Firebase Analytics. These events never include amounts, titles, notes or category names
  • Crash Reports and Diagnostics: Technical details when the app crashes or an error occurs, collected with Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device Identifiers: An app instance ID and the device's advertising ID, used by Firebase for analytics, and basic identifiers used by Google Play for app licensing

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties. Information is only processed by our service providers (Google Firebase and Google Play) on our behalf, or disclosed when legally required. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data Security

Your data security is our priority. Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS), and Google encrypts the data stored in Firebase at rest. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted. Local data is protected by your device's built-in security, and backups you create in the app are encrypted with a passphrase only you know. You can also turn on App Lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Data Deletion & Account Management

Full Control: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly through the in-app Delete Account option in Settings, or by requesting it on our Delete your account page. Once deleted, all synced data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Entries you delete in the app are also removed from the cloud.

Local Data: Data stored on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or through your device's settings.

Retention: Synced data is kept until you delete it or your account. Analytics and crash data are kept only for a limited time under Firebase's retention settings, after which Google deletes them automatically.

Third-Party Services

Google Firebase: Authentication (Google sign-in), Cloud Firestore (cloud sync for Pro), Analytics (usage analytics), Crashlytics (crash reports), Cloud Messaging (notifications) and Remote Config (feature settings).
Google Play Billing: Handles subscription payments securely. We never see your payment details.

App Lock and Biometrics

App Lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to ExpensePilot.

Children's Privacy

ExpensePilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy here and updating the effective date. Your continued use of the app indicates acceptance.

Contact Us

If you have privacy concerns or questions, please contact us at:
Email: support@expensepilot.in
Response Time: We typically respond within 7 business days.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access your data, request deletion, opt-out of data processing, and data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above.
