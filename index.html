Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

One clear answer on Home, and every number one tap away from the entries behind it.

Know what you can spend

  • Left to spend today, set at the start of the day. Tap "How?" to see exactly how it's worked out.
  • Money this month: what came in, what went out, what you kept, and when your next salary is due.
  • Monthly budgets with category limits, an "on track" signal and a warning at 80% of a limit.
  • Bills & subscriptions due soon on Home. Mark paid in one tap, or skip a month.
  • Savings goals: add money or take it out, and see what to put aside each month.

Log it in 3 seconds

  • Amount first, then one tap on a category chip. Title, payment method, note and date are optional.
  • Repeat last, a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts.
  • Expenses and income, decimals, and entries in a foreign currency.

Understand your money

  • Tap any number to see the entries behind it: a category, a day, a month, an income source.
  • This week or month: total and per day, money in vs out, where it went, the biggest expense, and whether you're on track.
  • A monthly recap you can share, and one useful insight on Home.
  • Search your activity, filter by type or payment method, swipe to delete with Undo.

Private and offline-first

  • Works fully offline. No account needed, no bank login, no SMS reading.
  • App lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Encrypted backup and restore, and CSV export.
  • Light and dark theme, any currency, in English, Hindi, Spanish and French.

Free & Pro

Free for every day. Pro for the deeper view.

The free app answers "what happened this period and am I OK?". Pro adds sync and the "why, compared to what, over time".

Free

No account needed. Everything stays on your phone.

  • Left to spend today, budgets and category limits
  • Expenses, income and foreign-currency entries
  • 3 bills and 3 goals
  • This week and month in Analytics, the monthly recap and insights
  • Widget, Quick Settings tile and shortcuts
  • App lock, encrypted backup and CSV export

Pro

PRO

A monthly subscription through Google Play.

  • Backup and sync of everything (entries, budgets, bills, limits and goals) across all your devices
  • Unlimited bills and goals
  • Year and custom ranges, a spending calendar, categories vs last period, six months of money in vs out
  • Income by source, fixed vs flexible spending, spending by payment method, your top 5 expenses
  • Advanced insights such as unusual spending and subscriptions spotted
  • PDF reports

About

Built for one question

Most expense apps show you charts of the past. ExpensePilot answers the question you actually have every day: how much can I safely spend today?

It is offline-first: your entries, budgets, bills and goals stay on your phone, and you can use the app without an account. If you want your data on more than one device, Pro syncs it through your Google account.

No ads, no bank login, no SMS reading. Just your numbers, kept simple.
